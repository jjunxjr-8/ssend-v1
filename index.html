100% Clickable Screen Guide

Left Screen Region (1/3 Width)

Cycles columns horizontally ($1 \to 2 \to 3 \to 4 \to 5$). Tap once to set Column 1.

Right Screen Region (1/3 Width)

Cycles rows vertically ($1 \to 2 \to 3 \to 4 \to 5$). Default is Row 1.

Center Screen Region (1/3 Width)

Prints letter at current target! If no column is selected, prints Space ' '. Automatically resets row & column after printing.

Left 1x + Center 1x → 'A'
Left 2x + Center 1x → 'B'
Right 1x + Left 1x + Center 1x → 'F'
